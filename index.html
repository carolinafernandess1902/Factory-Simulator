About the Project

Factory Simulator is an educational decision-support tool designed to explore how production capacity, workforce, demand, pricing and operating costs affect factory profitability.

The project combines concepts from Industrial Engineering, Economics, data analysis and programming to simulate simplified production decisions.

Methodology

Production

Production = Machines × Production Rate × Working Hours × Worker Efficiency

Units Sold

Units Sold = min(Production, Demand)

Revenue

Revenue = Units Sold × Selling Price

Total Costs

Total Costs = Worker Cost + Material Cost + Machine Cost

Profit

Profit = Revenue − Total Costs

Break-even Point

Break-even Units = (Worker Cost + Machine Cost) ÷ (Selling Price − Material Cost)

Workforce Optimization

The simulator evaluates different workforce sizes and compares their estimated daily profits to identify the configuration with the highest profit under the selected conditions.

This simulator uses simplified assumptions for educational purposes. It is not intended to represent a complete financial or operational model of a real factory.
